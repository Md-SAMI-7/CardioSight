6.2 Comparison of Federated Training Strategies

Per-class F1 standard deviation (F1-STD) across 12 diagnostic categories evaluating long-tail class-balance robustness.

FedCVD Robustness Metric

Table 5 presents the per-class F1 standard deviation (F1-STD) across the twelve diagnostic categories, using the long-tail robustness metric first introduced by FedCVD [1]. On CPSC-2018, FedProx achieves noticeably better class balance than FedAvg (0.2627 versus 0.3133), effectively exchanging raw accuracy for reduced disparity in performance on rare classes.

A further difference among the strategies concerns training stability: FedProx was unstable at μ = 0.01 and was therefore run with μ = 0.001 (Section 4.5.2).

Federated Optimization Frameworks

3 Strategies

FedAvg (Baseline)

Standard federated averaging aggregating local client weights proportionally to cohort sample size.

Global Aggregation: θt+1 = ∑ (nk/N) θk

FedProx (Proximal Term)

Introduces proximal regularization to restrict local model drift under severe non-IID class imbalance.

Proximal Parameter: μ = 0.001 (stabilized)

FedAdam (Adaptive Server)

Server-side adaptive momentum optimizer with first and second moment estimation for stable convergence.

Hyperparameters: β1 = 0.9, β2 = 0.99, η = 0.01, ε = 10-3

About CardioSight & Project Authors

Capstone Project under the School of Computer Science and Engineering (SCOPE), VIT-AP University.

Research Project Abstract

Multi-label cardiac arrhythmia detection using deep neural networks typically requires massive, centralized ECG datasets that compromise patient data privacy. We present CardioSight (ECG-FL-XAI), an uncertainty-aware, explainable federated learning framework trained across 5 geographically distributed clinical cohorts from the PhysioNet Challenge 2021.

Utilizing a 1D ResNet-34 backbone with adaptive federated optimizers (FedAvg, FedProx, FedAdam), we demonstrate that predictive uncertainty computed via Monte Carlo Dropout serves as a rigorous, verifiable safety metric correlated with inter-hospital explanation consistency (Grad-CAM and SHAP).
